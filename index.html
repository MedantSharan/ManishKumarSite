About Our Clinic

Welcome To Mind and Behaviour Clinic

At Mind and Behaviour Clinic, your mental well-being is our priority. Founded and personally managed by Dr. Manish Kumar, our clinic offers a compassionate and personalized approach to mental health care in the heart of Bihar.

Dr. Manish Kumar, a senior psychiatrist in Bihar, brings over 25 years of dedicated experience in mental health and psychiatry. With specialized expertise in adult psychiatry, adolescent psychiatry, deaddiction and many more, Dr. Kumar has helped countless individuals and families navigate their mental health journeys with empathy and professional excellence.

Whether you're seeking support for addiction, developmental disorders, sleep issues, or any mental health concern, you'll find a safe, non-judgmental space where healing begins. Dr. Kumar's patient-centered approach ensures that every individual receives the care and attention they deserve.
