About Us

Dr. Manish Kumar

MBBS DPM MD (Psychiatry)

MBBS from Patna Medical College (PMCH), Patna

DPM & MD in Psychiatry from Central Institute of Psychiatry (CIP), Kanke, Ranchi, Jharkhand

With over two decades of distinguished service in psychiatric care, Dr. Manish Kumar has emerged as one of Bihar's most respected mental health professionals. A native of Samastipur, Bihar, Dr. Kumar's journey in psychiatry has been marked by unwavering dedication to patient care and continuous professional excellence. He operates two comprehensive psychiatric facilities: his flagship Mind and Behaviour Clinic in Kankarbagh, Patna (G53, PC Colony, Near Manju Sinha Smriti Park), and a clinic in his hometown of Samastipur, ensuring accessible mental health care across the region. His extensive training from India's premier psychiatric institutions, combined with hands-on experience at nationally recognized hospitals, enables him to deliver evidence-based, compassionate treatment across the full spectrum of mental health conditions.
