About Dr. Manish Kumar

With over two decades of distinguished service in psychiatric care, Dr. Manish Kumar has emerged as one of Bihar's most respected mental health professionals. Dr. Kumar's journey in psychiatry has been marked by unwavering dedication to patient care and continuous professional excellence.

He operates his flagship Mind and Behaviour Clinic in Kankarbagh, Patna (G53, PC Colony, Near Manju Sinha Smriti Park), while also serving as a Senior Psychiatrist at Paras HMRI Hospital in Patna.

His extensive training from India's premier psychiatric institutions (MBBS from PMCH and MD Psychiatry from CIP Kanke, Jharkhand) combined with hands-on experience at nationally recognized hospitals, enables him to deliver evidence-based, compassionate treatment across the full spectrum of mental health conditions.
