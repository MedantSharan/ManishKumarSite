Our Services

Complete Mental Health Solutions

We offer comprehensive services for all your mental health requirements, including an in-house senior psychiatrist, specialized psychological therapies, a fully-equipped diagnostic laboratory, and a dedicated pharmacy. Mind and Behaviour Clinic is your one-stop destination for holistic mental healthcare. Explore the details of our services below.

Psychiatric Services

Dr. Manish Kumar, a senior psychiatrist and founder of Mind and Behaviour Clinic, directly offers various specialist psychiatric services. With over two decades of experience and training from premier institutions, Dr. Kumar provides evidence-based treatment for a comprehensive range of mental health conditions.

Adult and Child Psychiatry

+

Comprehensive psychiatric evaluation and treatment for both adults and children. Adult psychiatry addresses conditions such as mood disorders, anxiety, stress-related disorders, and psychotic illnesses. Child psychiatry focuses on developmental disorders, behavioral issues, attention deficit disorders, learning disabilities, and emotional problems in children and adolescents. Our approach is tailored to each individual's age, developmental stage, and unique circumstances.

Addiction Psychiatry & De-addiction

+

Specialized treatment for substance abuse and behavioral addictions including alcohol dependence, tobacco addiction, opioid use disorder, cannabis abuse, and other drug dependencies. Our de-addiction programs combine pharmacological interventions with counseling and behavioral therapies. We provide medically supervised detoxification, relapse prevention strategies, and ongoing support to help individuals achieve and maintain sobriety.

Adolescent Psychiatry

+

Dedicated mental health care for teenagers and young adults navigating the challenging adolescent years. We address issues such as peer pressure, academic stress, identity formation, mood swings, depression, anxiety, eating disorders, self-harm behaviors, and substance experimentation. Our adolescent-friendly approach creates a safe, non-judgmental environment where young people feel comfortable discussing their concerns.

Geriatric Psychiatry & Care

+

Specialized psychiatric care for elderly patients addressing age-related mental health concerns including dementia, Alzheimer's disease, late-life depression, anxiety disorders, sleep disturbances, and cognitive decline. We also provide support for caregivers and families dealing with the emotional and practical challenges of caring for elderly loved ones with mental health conditions.

Depression & Anxiety Disorders

+

Comprehensive treatment for major depressive disorder, persistent depressive disorder, generalized anxiety disorder, panic disorder, social anxiety, and phobias. Treatment includes evidence-based medications, psychotherapy, and lifestyle interventions. We help patients recognize symptoms, understand their condition, and develop effective coping strategies to regain control of their lives and improve overall well-being.

Schizophrenia

+

Expert management of schizophrenia and other psychotic disorders characterized by hallucinations, delusions, disorganized thinking, and impaired functioning. Our treatment approach combines antipsychotic medications with psychosocial interventions, family education, and rehabilitation programs. We focus on symptom management, preventing relapses, and helping patients achieve the highest possible level of independent functioning.

Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)

+

Specialized treatment for Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder involving intrusive thoughts (obsessions) and repetitive behaviors (compulsions). Treatment combines medication management with Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) therapy, the gold standard for OCD treatment. We help patients break the cycle of obsessions and compulsions, reduce anxiety, and develop healthier thought patterns and behaviors.

Sexual Disorders

+

Confidential and professional treatment for sexual dysfunctions including erectile dysfunction, premature ejaculation, low libido, arousal disorders, and orgasmic disorders. We also address psychological factors affecting sexual health such as performance anxiety, relationship issues, and past trauma. Our compassionate, non-judgmental approach helps patients overcome difficulties and improve sexual well-being and relationship satisfaction.

Epilepsy Care & Management

+

Comprehensive diagnosis and management of epilepsy and seizure disorders with over 25 years of clinical experience. Services include detailed seizure history assessment, diagnostic investigations, personalized medication management, lifestyle counseling, and long-term monitoring. We help patients achieve optimal seizure control while minimizing medication side effects and maximizing quality of life.

Headaches & Migraines

+

Expert evaluation and treatment for chronic headaches, migraines, tension-type headaches, and cluster headaches. We identify triggers, provide acute and preventive treatment strategies, and offer lifestyle modifications to reduce frequency and severity of headaches. Treatment may include medications, stress management techniques, and identification of dietary or environmental triggers.

Developmental Pediatrics

+

Specialized assessment and management of developmental disorders in children including autism spectrum disorder, attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), intellectual disabilities, learning disorders, and developmental delays. We provide comprehensive developmental evaluations, early intervention recommendations, behavioral management strategies, and family counseling to support optimal child development.

Psychological Services

We have an in-house psychologist who offers various forms of specialized therapies and psychological evaluations. Our psychological services complement psychiatric treatment and provide essential therapeutic interventions for mental health and behavioral concerns.

Psychometric Evaluations & Testing

+

Comprehensive psychological assessments using standardized tests to evaluate intelligence (IQ testing), personality traits, cognitive abilities, academic achievement, neuropsychological functioning, and career aptitude. Common evaluations include WAIS, WISC, Rorschach test, MMPI, TAT, and specialized assessments for ADHD, autism, learning disabilities, and dementia. Results guide diagnosis, treatment planning, and educational/occupational recommendations.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

+

Evidence-based psychotherapy that focuses on identifying and changing negative thought patterns and behaviors. CBT is highly effective for depression, anxiety disorders, panic attacks, PTSD, eating disorders, and insomnia. Patients learn practical skills to challenge distorted thinking, develop healthier behaviors, and solve problems effectively. Treatment is goal-oriented and typically shows results within 12-20 sessions.

Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) Therapy

+

Gold standard treatment for Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD) and related conditions. ERP involves gradual exposure to anxiety-provoking situations while preventing the compulsive response. Through repeated practice, patients learn that anxiety decreases naturally without performing compulsions. Also effective for phobias, contamination fears, and other anxiety-based disorders. Treatment is structured, systematic, and produces lasting results.

Relaxation Therapy & Stress Management

+

Techniques to reduce physical and mental tension including progressive muscle relaxation, deep breathing exercises, guided imagery, and mindfulness practices. These methods help manage stress, reduce anxiety, improve sleep quality, lower blood pressure, and enhance overall well-being. Patients learn portable skills that can be practiced anywhere to achieve calm and relaxation in daily life.

Anger Management Therapy

+

Structured therapy to help individuals recognize anger triggers, understand underlying emotions, and develop healthier ways to express and manage anger. Techniques include cognitive restructuring, relaxation training, communication skills, and problem-solving strategies. Beneficial for those experiencing relationship conflicts, workplace issues, or legal problems related to anger outbursts. Improves emotional regulation and interpersonal relationships.

General Counseling & Supportive Therapy

+

Professional counseling for life challenges, relationship issues, grief and loss, career decisions, self-esteem concerns, and adjustment difficulties. Provides a safe, confidential space to explore feelings, gain insights, and develop coping strategies. Supportive therapy helps individuals navigate difficult times, make important decisions, and improve overall psychological well-being without necessarily treating diagnosed mental disorders.

Laboratory & Pharmacy Services

We have a dedicated in-house diagnostic laboratory and pharmacy to provide complete medical support for your mental health treatment. Our laboratory is equipped with modern equipment for essential diagnostic tests, and our pharmacy stocks all necessary psychiatric and neurological medications for immediate access after consultation.

Complete Blood Count (CBC)

+

Comprehensive blood test measuring red blood cells, white blood cells, hemoglobin, hematocrit, and platelets. Essential for baseline health assessment before starting psychiatric medications, monitoring for medication side effects, detecting anemia, and identifying infections or blood disorders. Results help ensure safe medication management and overall health monitoring during psychiatric treatment.

Liver Function Test (LFT)

+

Blood tests measuring liver enzymes (ALT, AST, ALP), bilirubin, albumin, and total protein to assess liver health and function. Critical before prescribing medications metabolized by the liver and for regular monitoring during treatment with mood stabilizers, antipsychotics, and other psychiatric medications. Helps detect liver damage, monitor alcohol-related problems, and ensure medication safety.

Kidney Function Test (KFT)

+

Tests measuring blood urea nitrogen (BUN), serum creatinine, and electrolytes to evaluate kidney function. Essential before starting lithium therapy and for regular monitoring of patients on medications that may affect kidney function. Helps detect kidney disease, monitor medication side effects, and adjust medication dosages based on kidney function to ensure patient safety.

Serum Lithium & Drug Assays

+

Specialized blood tests measuring lithium levels and other psychiatric medication levels including mood stabilizers, antiepileptic drugs, and certain antipsychotics. Regular monitoring is crucial as lithium has a narrow therapeutic window. Testing ensures optimal dosing, prevents dangerous side effects, maintains treatment efficacy, and enables personalized medication management based on individual metabolism.

Thyroid Function & Blood Sugar Tests

+

Thyroid function tests (TSH, T3, T4) and blood sugar tests (fasting glucose, HbA1c) to evaluate endocrine function. Important as thyroid disorders can mimic psychiatric symptoms, and some psychiatric medications can affect glucose and lipid metabolism. Regular monitoring enables early intervention, lifestyle modifications, and medication adjustments to prevent metabolic complications.

Electrocardiogram (ECG)

+

Non-invasive test recording the electrical activity of the heart. Essential before starting medications that may affect cardiac function, particularly tricyclic antidepressants, antipsychotics, and mood stabilizers. Regular ECG monitoring helps detect cardiac side effects early, ensuring patient safety. Especially important for elderly patients and those with existing heart conditions.

+

Our dedicated in-house pharmacy stocks a comprehensive range of psychiatric medications (antidepressants, antianxiety medications, mood stabilizers, antipsychotics), neurological medications (antiepileptic drugs, migraine treatments), de-addiction medications, sleep aids, and supportive medications.
